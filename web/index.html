    Connection log
      Connect over Wi-Fi (no cable)
      1. Trust this Mac over USB, once. Plug the iPhone in, unlock it, and click Trust & prepare (tap Trust on the phone).
      2. Turn on Wi-Fi connections while the cable is still plugged in.
      3. Same Wi-Fi network. Put the iPhone and this Mac on the same Wi-Fi. Guest networks and some work/school networks block devices from seeing each other.
      4. Unplug the cable. Within a few seconds the iPhone shows a Wi-Fi badge here. Keep it unlocked the first time; if it doesn't appear, wake the phone and click ↻.

      USB is the most reliable connection. Over Wi-Fi the iPhone can drop off when it sleeps for a long time or changes networks. The app reconnects and re-applies your location automatically.
